🔗 How to get Dhan API credentials
1. Log in to your Dhan account at dhan.co
2. Go to Profile → API (or search "API" in settings)
3. Click "Create API" or "Generate Token"
4. Copy your Client ID and Access Token
5. Paste them above and click "Save & Connect"
6. Click "Test Connection" to verify it works
7. Switch to Live mode when you are confident
